Industrial Waste Treatment

The oil, mining, and tourism industries in the United States and Canada face intense levels of public and regulatory scrutiny. Lifewater's ExtremeSTP® wastewater treatment plants provide:

  • High effluent quality
  • Satisfies all discharge requirements
  • Easy transport
  • Easy installation
  • SCADA System for remote monitoring via internet or mounted satellite dish
  • Real time operational assistance and training via remote communication
  • Certified operator oversight
  • State certified plan for your operator
  • Time and money savings over the life of our systems

All technologies can be manufactured in various configurations, including inside shipping containers to facilitate transport to a site. Larger systems can be placed inside a heated building. Summer use only systems can be used outdoors in uninsulated configurations.

Portable Systems

Lifewater's portable systems are suitable for camps including those used for mineral exploration, ice road construction, oil/gas exploration drilling, road construction or maintenance, spill cleanup, environmental remediation, etc.

Our portable sewage treatment plants use side stream, tubular, cross-flow, polymeric, membrane bioreactor (MBR) treatment technology, which offers the following advantages:

  • Small footprint
  • Membranes resistant to transport vibrations and shocks
  • Membranes inside pipes — no handling of dirty membranes
  • Easy winterization for freezing temperatures with very little antifreeze
  • Simple startup and shutdown
  • All operation & service within the systems enclosure (20 or 40 ft container)

Permanent Systems

For non-portable treatment plants we use the best treatment technology for your application. Many technologies are possible but the ones that we typically choose from include:

Submerged Fixed Film Activated Sludge

  • Low energy use
  • Very simple technology to operate and maintain

Immersed, Polymeric Flat Plate MBRs

  • Low energy use
  • Relatively lightweight

Sidestream, Cross-Flow, Polymeric Tubular MBRs

  • Small footprint
  • Resistant to vibrations and transport shocks
  • Membranes inside pipes — no dirty membrane handling
  • Easy winterization and long-term storage
  • All operation within container enclosure

Immersed, Ceramic Flat Plate MBRs

  • 20-year projected life
  • Low energy use
  • Not damaged by drying out or freezing
  • Can withstand backpressure for cleaning
  • Resistant to nearly all chemicals and heat
