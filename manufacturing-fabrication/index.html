Manufacturing & Fabrication

From custom plastic fabrication and CNC routing to containerized equipment solutions — we bring your ideas to life using the best tools and techniques available.

Product Design

Our team is available to help you bring your ideas and solutions into reality. By using 3D CAD to aid in the design process, we can digitally create the part that can be easily transformed into a real object.

Plastic Fabrication

Our certified plastic welders can take on the simplest of tasks or more complex projects. These tasks can range from a simple battery cover to a complex water tank with structural supports.

Our team uses many tools to aid this process, including 3D CAD, 3-axis CNC router, various saws, extrusion welders, and butt fusion equipment.

Our Capabilities:

  • 3D CAD design
  • 3-axis CNC router
  • Certified plastic welders
  • Extrusion welding
  • Butt fusion equipment
  • Variety of plastic materials

Containerizing Your Equipment

Our team at Lifewater has designed and tested solutions that will allow your equipment to operate in the Arctic, Antarctic, or anywhere on earth.

Some of our solutions include modifying standardized shipping containers to meet your needs. These can be stand alone units or modularized units tied together with plumbing and electrical.

We also specialize in building custom enclosures designed to meet your specifications, including custom skid frames, insulation & ventilation packages, and remote monitoring systems.

Container Solutions Include:

  • Modified shipping containers (20 ft & 40 ft)
  • Stand-alone or modular units
  • Plumbing & electrical integration
  • Custom skid frames
  • Insulation & ventilation packages
  • Remote monitoring systems

CNC Router Machining

A CNC router can custom machine plastic, wood, foam, or aluminum. Applications can include mill-work, exhibits and fixtures, cabinetry, and sign making.

Our table space has 5 feet × 10 feet of availability, enabling us to machine ultra thin material up to 3 inches thick. Our router is equipped with a 12-position tool holder which allows for a variety of cutting tools available to the machine.

CNC Router Specs:

  • Table space: 5 ft × 10 ft
  • Material thickness: up to 3 inches
  • 12-position tool holder
  • Materials: plastic, wood, foam, aluminum
  • Applications: mill-work, exhibits, cabinetry, signage
