Residential & Commercial Technology

We use residential and commercial technology for our village & subdivision systems. Please contact us for assistance in determining what type of system best fits your needs.

For permanent treatment plants we frequently recommend ceramic flat plate membranes. The advantages of this type of ExtremeSTP® sewage treatment plant include:

  • Small footprint
  • Ceramic membranes with a projected 20-year lifetime
  • Very little electrical energy usage
  • Can withstand backpressure during cleaning
  • Consistently produce top quality effluent (typically near the detection limits for BOD5 and TSS)

We also work with individuals and housing authorities.

Emergency Response Systems

Our portable systems are well suited for use in emergency situations such as flooding, erosion, and earthquakes — providing rapid deployment capabilities to communities in need.
