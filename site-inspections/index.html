Site Inspections & Service Contracts

Harsh winters and warm summers can put a strain on your Wastewater Treatment plant. Our technicians ensure your system receives the best care possible.

Our technicians at Lifewater offer site inspections to residential homes and commercial properties, ensuring the best care for your system.

This Inspection Includes:

  • Place the system into the proper operation mode (winter/summer) for the upcoming season
  • Observe and note the overall condition of the treatment plant
  • Check and clean the blower air filter, as necessary
  • Check and clean the blower housing, as necessary
  • Check the function of the linear air compressor
  • Check and clean the air diffuser, as necessary
  • Check and clean the UV bulb and housing, as necessary
  • Inform the owner of work needing to be done not covered under warranty or service contract

Lifewater Engineering Company offers Annual Servicing Contracts to residential homes to help with the upkeep of your tank. Every Spring and Fall, someone from our team will come out for a site visit to thoroughly inspect your wastewater system.

Our mission is to provide high-quality service for environmentally sound living in cold climates. We offer our professional services, and hands-on training, to ensure quality control while maintaining your Lifewater system.

Annual Service Contracts Include:

  • Place the system into the proper operation mode (winter/summer)
  • Observe and note the overall condition of the treatment plant
  • Check and clean the blower air filter, as necessary
  • Check and clean the blower housing, as necessary
  • Check the function of the linear air compressor
  • Check and clean the air diffuser, as necessary
  • Check and clean the UV bulb and housing, as necessary
  • Report on work needing to be done not covered under warranty

Note: If the system needs pumping out (every 2–3 years), this is not included in the service contract, but can be provided for an additional cost.
