About Lifewater

Founded on Innovation

Lifewater Engineering Company was founded as an environmental engineering consulting company in Fairbanks, Alaska. We transitioned into a manufacturing company in 1999 when a client asked us to design a sewage treatment system for his house on permafrost.

Using 3-D design software, a CNC router, and plastic welding equipment, we create functional end products that are founded on innovation. Our goal is to listen well, select the most suitable equipment for your project, then design, build, and provide all the technical support needed to make it a success.

Because we do the design and manufacturing ourselves, your project moves ahead quickly. Since many northern sites and communities are not connected by roads, we commonly design treatment systems for fixed wing and helicopter transport.

"Providing top quality wastewater treatment for a healthier world."

Products

Built for Extreme Conditions

Every product is designed, manufactured, and tested in Fairbanks to operate reliably at temperatures down to -60°F.

ExtremeSTP® Aboveground Systems

Double-walled, fully insulated tanks designed for outdoor installation above the ground surface. Ideal for areas where permafrost, bedrock, or soil conditions make belowground installation impractical.

  • Operates at -60°F (-53°C) with reliable power
  • Available with built-in inlet lift station
  • 300 and 600 gallon capacities
  • Optional thermosyphon for permafrost protection

Cold Climate Innovation

Engineered for the Harshest Environments on Earth

Anyone living and working in the Arctic and sub-Arctic knows how cold it can get. We design and manufacture systems that work in this harsh and fragile environment. Our aboveground systems will not freeze in arctic cold or crack in desert heat.

We have a research and development relationship with the Cold Climate Housing Research Center at the University of Alaska Fairbanks, developing low-energy systems for sustainable villages and communities.
