Residential Sewage Treatment Plants

Aboveground and belowground ExtremeSTP® systems designed for Arctic conditions — sized to be aircraft transportable to remote northern communities.

Starting at $28,500 (300 gal) — $41,250 (600 gal)

Lifewater Engineering Company offers aboveground and belowground ExtremeSTP® residential sewage treatment plants. Aboveground units are specifically designed for areas where permafrost, bedrock or other ground or soil conditions make it impractical to install a belowground system.

For the aboveground systems, the preferred location for discharge is to undisturbed, natural vegetation such as a black spruce forest floor, the tundra surface, or a peat bog.

Higher capacity systems are available for larger homes and apartment buildings. For an alternative to a sewage treatment system, please see our Separett® waterless toilets.

Key Benefits:

  • Operates at −60°F (−53°C)
  • Does not corrode, never needs painting
  • Aircraft transportable
  • Effluent suitable for surface discharge

Standard Aboveground STPs

Double-walled, fully insulated tank designed to be installed outdoors above the ground surface. Available with or without a built-in inlet lift station. All STPs have an effluent pump. Drawings for a simple treated timber foundation are available.

Uninsulated Aboveground STPs

Available for installation inside a heated enclosure.

Standard Belowground STPs

Single-walled, fully insulated tank designed to be installed below the ground surface with 4 inches to 4 feet of soil cover. Every system has an effluent pump.

Optional Equipment

  • Built-in lift station (to lift raw sewage up into an aboveground treatment plant)
  • External arctic outdoor lift station (mounted to the outside of a house or set partially into the ground)
  • Thermosyphon (to keep the permafrost beneath the system frozen)
